# Disclosure of Body-Composition Data to the Coach — Supplemental Consent Terms

_Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-05-18_

These Supplemental Terms govern Participant's optional consent to disclose body-composition and related condition data ("Sensitive Health Data") to the designated **Coach** role within ShuttleScope. They operate in addition to (and do not modify) the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md), the [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md), and the participant consent instrument used at enrollment.

Disclosure of Sensitive Health Data to the Coach is **expressly optional**. By default, the Coach receives only the three-band composite indicator ("good / caution / requires attention") and never the raw values. Because of the asymmetric authority that a coach holds over playing time, selection, and career trajectory, Operator strongly recommends that Participant consider declining this consent unless there is a specific reason to grant it.

## 1. Scope of "Sensitive Health Data"

As defined in §1 of the Analyst Disclosure Supplemental Terms: body-composition metrics, raw 44-item questionnaire scores, raw Hooper Index components, raw RPE values. It does not include the three-band composite indicator.

## 2. Permitted Use by the Coach

The Coach may use disclosed Sensitive Health Data **solely** for:

1. Tailoring training intensity, load, and recovery scheduling.
2. Coordinating with sports-medicine or strength-and-conditioning staff engaged by the team where Participant has separately consented to such onward sharing.
3. Confidential coaching conversations with Participant about training response.

The Coach is **expressly prohibited** from using Sensitive Health Data for:

- Selection or de-selection decisions framed in terms of body composition.
- Public, semi-public, or peer-visible commentary on Participant's body, weight, or eating behaviour.
- Disclosure to teammates, opposing teams, parents (where Participant is an adult), sponsors, or media.
- Any disciplinary purpose.

## 3. Recommended Defaults

Operator recommends that Participant **does not** grant this consent unless:

- Participant has independently chosen the Coach for body-composition-aware coaching, and
- Participant has a means of revoking the consent quickly and without adverse social consequence within the team environment.

This recommendation is informational and does not bind Participant's decision.

## 4. Logging and Notification

All Coach access to Sensitive Health Data is logged on the same basis as Analyst access (§4 of the Analyst Disclosure Supplemental Terms). Where Operator detects an anomalous access pattern (e.g., bulk export, repeated access outside reasonable hours), Operator will notify Participant.

## 5. Withdrawal

Participant may withdraw this consent at any time, by any means that reasonably communicates the withdrawal. Withdrawal takes effect upon Operator's receipt and is enforced through in-System access controls without undue delay. Participant is not required to give a reason and shall not suffer adverse treatment from Operator on account of withdrawal.

## 6. No Medical Use

As with the Analyst Disclosure Supplemental Terms, Sensitive Health Data are not clinical-grade and must not be used as a substitute for professional medical assessment or sports-medicine clearance.

## 7. Governing Law

Governed by the laws of Japan; Tokyo District Court has exclusive jurisdiction, without prejudice to mandatory consumer-protection rules of Participant's domicile.
