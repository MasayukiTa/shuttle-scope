# Disclosure of Body-Composition Data to the Team Analyst — Supplemental Consent Terms

_Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-05-18_

These Supplemental Terms govern Participant's optional consent to disclose body-composition and related condition data ("Sensitive Health Data") to the designated team **Analyst** role within ShuttleScope. They operate in addition to (and do not modify) the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md), the [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md), and the participant consent instrument used at enrollment. Consent is **optional**; absent consent, Sensitive Health Data remain visible only to Participant and to Operator in the technical-administration capacity described in §5 of the consent instrument.

## 1. Scope of "Sensitive Health Data"

For the purposes of these Supplemental Terms, Sensitive Health Data means:

- Body weight, body-fat percentage, skeletal-muscle mass, basal metabolic rate, segmental composition, and any other metric produced by InBody or equivalent body-composition measurement instruments.
- Raw scores from the 44-item subjective condition questionnaire used by the System.
- Raw Hooper Index components (fatigue, stress, mood, sleep).
- Raw RPE (Rating of Perceived Exertion) values.

It does **not** include the three-band composite indicator ("good / caution / requires attention") which the System discloses to the Analyst role independently under §5 of the consent instrument.

## 2. Permitted Use by the Analyst

The Analyst may use disclosed Sensitive Health Data solely for:

1. Longitudinal performance analysis correlating physical condition with on-court metrics.
2. Preparation of analyst-facing reports for internal team discussion.
3. Flagging of anomalies (e.g., sustained drops in muscle mass) for follow-up with Participant or Operator.

The Analyst is **not** authorised to:

- Forward Sensitive Health Data to coaches, opponents, parents, sponsors, medical practitioners not engaged by Participant, or the public.
- Discuss Sensitive Health Data in any setting where third parties may overhear or observe analyst displays.
- Use Sensitive Health Data to derive judgments of moral character, discipline, body image, eating behaviour, or fitness-for-selection.

## 3. Operator's Role

Operator implements the access-control mechanisms that gate Analyst visibility of Sensitive Health Data. Operator does not separately re-disclose Sensitive Health Data on Analyst's behalf and is not responsible for downstream conduct of the Analyst beyond enforcing in-System access controls and logging.

## 4. Logging

All Analyst access to Sensitive Health Data is logged with timestamp, accessing-account identifier, and queried data scope. Logs are retained for not less than twelve (12) months and are available to Participant on request.

## 5. Withdrawal

Participant may withdraw this consent at any time, in any manner that reasonably communicates the withdrawal. Withdrawal takes effect upon Operator's receipt and is propagated to in-System access controls without undue delay. Analyses already produced from previously disclosed Sensitive Health Data need not be retroactively recalled, but the underlying data will no longer be accessible to the Analyst going forward.

## 6. No Medical Use

Sensitive Health Data exposed under these Supplemental Terms are **not** clinical-grade and must not be used by the Analyst, Operator, or any other party as a substitute for professional medical assessment, diagnosis, treatment, or sports-medicine clearance.

## 7. Governing Law

Governed by the laws of Japan; Tokyo District Court has exclusive jurisdiction, without prejudice to mandatory consumer-protection rules of Participant's domicile.
