# Academic Research Use — Supplemental Consent Terms

_Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-05-18_

These Supplemental Terms govern the optional use of data collected through ShuttleScope for academic research purposes, in addition to (and without modifying) the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md) and the [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md). Consent under these Supplemental Terms is **strictly optional**: declining or later withdrawing has no effect on Participant's ability to use the System for ordinary match-analysis purposes.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Research Materials"** means any Match Data, Posture Data, Condition Data, or Body-Composition Data contributed by Participant and selected by Operator for inclusion in a research dataset.
- **"Research Outputs"** means academic papers, posters, theses, conference presentations, preprints, blog posts of a scholarly nature, and underlying analysis code or anonymised datasets prepared in connection with a Research Activity.
- **"Research Activity"** means any study, modelling exercise, methodological evaluation, or statistical investigation conducted by Operator or by collaborators acting under written agreement with Operator.

## 2. Permitted Research Purposes

Research Materials may be used solely for the following purposes:

1. Modelling of Expected Point Value (EPV), win-probability, or related shot-quality metrics in badminton.
2. Statistical or machine-learning research on athlete condition, fatigue, perceived exertion (RPE), or Hooper Index-style composite indicators.
3. Methodological research on differential privacy, k-anonymity, federated learning, or other privacy-preserving techniques applied to athlete data.
4. Evaluation of computer-vision pipelines (pose estimation, shuttle tracking, court calibration) on real-world match footage.
5. Reproducibility and benchmarking work supporting (1)–(4).

Any use materially outside the foregoing requires fresh, specific consent.

## 3. Anonymisation and Re-Identification

Operator will, prior to any external disclosure of Research Materials or Research Outputs:

- Remove direct identifiers (name, email, organisational affiliation).
- Replace stable identifiers with pseudonymous keys held separately from the analytic dataset.
- Aggregate or perturb low-cell-count statistics where re-identification risk is non-trivial.
- Apply differential-privacy noise to released summary statistics where appropriate to the publication venue.

Participant acknowledges that **complete elimination of re-identification risk is not technically guaranteed**, particularly where Participant is a member of a small cohort (e.g., a single team).

## 4. Governance

Each Research Activity will be conducted only after:

1. Internal review by Operator's supervisor or designated reviewer within Operator's employing organisation; or
2. Approval by a recognised institutional review board / ethics committee, where the venue or collaborator requires it.

Operator will maintain a written record of the review outcome, the scope of approved use, and the Research Outputs produced.

## 5. Pre-Publication Notice

Where a Research Output identifies a specific cohort to which Participant belongs (even pseudonymously), Operator will provide Participant with reasonable advance notice and an opportunity to raise objections prior to public release. Operator will consider objections in good faith but is not obliged to suppress publication where the cohort is sufficiently de-identified.

## 6. Withdrawal

Participant may withdraw consent at any time by contacting Operator at the address in §10 of the [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY.md). Upon withdrawal:

- Research Materials not yet incorporated into an analytic dataset will be excluded.
- Materials already incorporated into analyses, models, or accepted publications need not be retroactively removed where doing so would be technically infeasible or scientifically dishonest (e.g., would invalidate a published result).
- Operator will record the withdrawal date and exclude further contributions of Research Materials going forward.

## 7. No Compensation

Participation in Research Activities is voluntary and uncompensated. Participant retains no commercial claim against any Research Output or any model parameter derived from Research Materials.
