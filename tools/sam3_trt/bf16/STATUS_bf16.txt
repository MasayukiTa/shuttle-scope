ROOT CAUSE OF FP16/BF16 COLLAPSE:
  Catastrophic (corr 0.11 = noise), byte-identical across plain-fp16, bf16, and
  every "matmul-fp32 but elementwise-fp16" variant. The poison is the ATTENTION
  QK/AV score matmuls + RoPE rotation in reduced precision. bf16's wider exponent
  did NOT help. ALL dense linear projections (qkv, proj, mlp fc1/fc2) tolerate fp16
  perfectly (rel 0.0065). Only attention-core scores + RoPE + LN must stay fp32.

BEST FAST+NUMERICALLY-CORRECT ENGINE: bf16/sam3_enc_linfp16.plan
  encoder rel 0.0065, corr 0.99993, 118.3ms (1.20x vs 141.9ms FP32). 883 MB. VRAM e2e 4852MB.

BUT END-TO-END MASK IoU FAILS THE SPIRIT OF THE TARGET:
  linfp16 hybrid frame0: 71 PT masks vs 67 hybrid (4 DROPPED).
  mean best-IoU 0.929 (>0.9) BUT min 0.172. Several small/marginal person masks
  badly degraded (0.17, 0.41, 0.53...). The 0.65% encoder perturbation is amplified
  by the SAM3 mask decoder on borderline detections -> dropped/degraded masks.
  FP32 baseline on same harness: min IoU 0.976, no dropped masks.

  A 1.20x-faster encoder (linfp16) exists that passes rel-err<1e-2 AND mean IoU>0.9,

  person tracking where small/distant detections matter. FP32+noTactic

  fp16 is only acceptable if reduced small-object mask recall is tolerable.
