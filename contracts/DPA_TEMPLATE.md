# ShuttleScope Data Processing Agreement Template
## Version 1.0 — Template; not effective unless executed by the parties

---

### PREAMBLE

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of, and is supplemental to,
the agreement between the Controller and the Processor under which the
Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller in connection
with the ShuttleScope software application ("Software") and any associated
services. This DPA gives effect to the requirements of Article 28(3) of
Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and to comparable obligations under the UK
GDPR, the Act on the Protection of Personal Information of Japan ("APPI"), and
other applicable national data protection legislation, to the extent
applicable to the engagement.

This DPA is provided as a template. Parties intending to execute it shall
insert the particulars indicated in square brackets, complete Annexes 1
through 4, sign the execution block, and retain countersigned copies as a
condition to the Processor's engagement under the principal agreement.

---

### ARTICLE 2 — SUBJECT MATTER AND DURATION OF PROCESSING

**2.1 Subject Matter.**  
The subject matter of the Processing under this DPA is the operation,
analysis, and improvement of the Software in respect of badminton match data,
athlete performance data, and ancillary data categories described in Annex 1.

**2.2 Duration.**  
This DPA shall remain in effect for the duration of the principal agreement
and shall continue thereafter to the extent that any of the obligations herein
require continued performance, including without limitation deletion or return
of Personal Data.

---

### ARTICLE 3 — NATURE AND PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

**3.1 Nature.**  
The Processor shall Process Personal Data to deliver the Software's
annotation, analysis, reporting, visualization, and synchronization functions
as described in the Software's documentation and the Controller's instructions.

**3.2 Purpose.**  
The purpose of the Processing is the provision of badminton performance
analytics services to the Controller for the benefit of the Controller's
athletes, coaching staff, and authorized analysts.

---

### ARTICLE 4 — CATEGORIES OF DATA AND DATA SUBJECTS

**4.1 Categories of Personal Data.**  
The categories of Personal Data Processed under this DPA are described in
Annex 1.

**4.2 Categories of Data Subjects.**  
The Data Subjects whose Personal Data is Processed under this DPA include:

  **(a)** athletes whose performance is recorded, annotated, or analyzed
  through the Software;
  **(b)** coaching staff, analysts, and other authorized Users of the
  Software acting on behalf of the Controller;
  **(c)** any other natural person whose Personal Data the Controller submits
  to the Software.

### ARTICLE 5 — OBLIGATIONS OF THE PROCESSOR

**5.1 Processing on Documented Instructions.**  
The Processor shall Process Personal Data only on the documented instructions
of the Controller, including with regard to transfers of Personal Data to a
third country or an international organization, unless required to do so by
applicable law to which the Processor is subject. In such a case, the
Processor shall inform the Controller of that legal requirement before
Processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds
of public interest.

**5.2 Confidentiality.**  
The Processor shall ensure that persons authorized to Process the Personal
Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate
statutory obligation of confidentiality.

**5.3 Security Measures.**  
The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational
measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk pursuant to
Article 32 GDPR, including without limitation the measures described in
Annex 2.

**5.4 Sub-processor Engagement.**  
The Processor may engage Sub-processors only:

  **(a)** subject to the Controller's prior specific or general written
  authorization, provided that in the case of general written authorization
  the Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended addition or
  replacement of Sub-processors and afford the Controller a reasonable
  opportunity to object to such changes;

  **(b)** subject to a written agreement imposing on the Sub-processor data
  protection obligations no less protective than those set out in this DPA;
  and

  **(c)** as listed in Annex 3 (which the parties shall update from time to
  time as Sub-processor engagements change).

**5.5 Data Subject Rights Assistance.**  
Taking into account the nature of the Processing, the Processor shall assist
the Controller by appropriate technical and organizational measures, insofar
as possible, for the fulfillment of the Controller's obligation to respond to
requests for exercising Data Subject rights under Chapter III GDPR.

**5.6 Breach Notification.**  
The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming
aware of a Personal Data breach. Such notification shall include, to the
extent reasonably available at the time:

  **(a)** the nature of the breach including, where possible, the categories
  and approximate number of Data Subjects and Personal Data records concerned;
  **(b)** the likely consequences of the breach;
  **(c)** the measures taken or proposed to address the breach.

**5.7 DPIA Assistance.**  
The Processor shall assist the Controller in carrying out data protection
impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities under
Articles 35 and 36 GDPR, taking into account the nature of the Processing and
the information available to the Processor.

**5.8 Return or Deletion.**  
Upon termination of the principal agreement or upon the Controller's earlier
request, the Processor shall, at the Controller's choice, return or delete
all Personal Data Processed on behalf of the Controller and delete existing
copies, save where applicable law requires retention by the Processor.

**5.9 Audit Rights.**  
The Processor shall make available to the Controller all information
reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA, and shall allow
for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the
Controller or another auditor mandated by the Controller. Audit rights shall
be exercised on reasonable prior notice, no more than once per twelve-month
period absent reasonable cause, and subject to confidentiality obligations
appropriate to the Processor's business.

### ARTICLE 7 — INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

**7.1 Adequacy Decision Reliance.**  
Where Personal Data is transferred from the European Economic Area to a
country in respect of which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy
decision under Article 45 GDPR, such transfer shall be made on the basis of
that decision.

**7.2 Standard Contractual Clauses.**  
Where Personal Data is transferred to a country not subject to an adequacy
decision, the parties shall execute the Standard Contractual Clauses set out
in Annex 4 (or shall be deemed to have done so, where the parties have
already executed equivalent clauses in connection with the principal
agreement). The parties shall implement supplementary technical and
organizational measures appropriate to the risk profile of the transfer and
shall conduct a transfer impact assessment where required by applicable
guidance.

**7.3 Cross-Border Provision under APPI.**  
Where Personal Data subject to the APPI is provided to the Processor located
outside Japan, the Controller shall confirm that one of the following bases
applies:

  **(a)** the recipient country has a personal information protection system
  recognized by the Personal Information Protection Commission of Japan as
  providing protection equivalent to that under the APPI;

  **(b)** the recipient implements equivalent protective measures (which the
  Processor undertakes to maintain in conformity with this DPA); or

  **(c)** the Data Subject has consented to the cross-border provision after
  being informed of the relevant country's personal information protection
  system.

---

### ARTICLE 8 — LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY

**8.1 General.**  
Each party's liability under or in connection with this DPA shall be subject
to the limitations and exclusions of liability set out in the principal
agreement.

**8.2 Allocation Among Joint Defendants.**  
Where the Controller and the Processor are joint defendants in proceedings
arising from a Processing activity under this DPA, the parties shall
cooperate in good faith on the conduct of the defense and shall allocate
liability between themselves in proportion to each party's responsibility
for the alleged violation, taking into account each party's role under
Articles 5(2) and 24-28 GDPR.

---

### ARTICLE 9 — TERM AND TERMINATION

**9.1 Term.**  
This DPA shall enter into force on the effective date of the principal
agreement and shall continue until the termination of the principal
agreement.

**9.2 Survival.**  
The provisions of Articles 5.8 (return or deletion), 5.9 (audit rights to
the extent applicable to retained data), and 8 (liability and indemnity)
shall survive the termination of this DPA.

---

### ARTICLE 10 — GENERAL PROVISIONS

**10.1 Order of Precedence.**  
In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the principal agreement, this
DPA shall prevail with respect to the matters governed by it. Where the
parties have executed the Standard Contractual Clauses included in Annex 4,
those clauses shall prevail over conflicting provisions of this DPA with
respect to international transfers covered by them.

**10.2 Governing Law.**  
This DPA shall be governed by the law specified in the principal agreement,
provided that, with respect to data protection matters arising under the
GDPR, the protections afforded by Articles 7, 12-22, and 79 GDPR shall not
be diminished by such choice of law.

**10.3 Severability.**  
If any provision of this DPA is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining
provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**10.4 Counterparts.**  
This DPA may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an
original and all of which together shall constitute one and the same
instrument.

## ANNEX 1 — PROCESSING DETAILS

**Subject matter and duration:** As specified in the principal agreement.

**Nature and purpose of the Processing:**

The Processor's Processing of Personal Data on the Controller's behalf
includes the following operations performed in connection with the Software:

  1. ingestion and storage of match video files submitted by the Controller;
  2. computer-vision-based annotation of match video, including shuttle
     trajectory tracking, pose estimation, and shot classification;
  3. derivation of analytical metrics including expected point value,
     win-rate estimates, court coverage statistics, and similar quantitative
     summaries;
  4. presentation of analysis results through the Software's user interface
     to authorized Users designated by the Controller;
  5. operational logging, security monitoring, and incident response
     activities incidental to service delivery.

**Categories of Personal Data:**

  1. athlete identifiers and performance metadata (name, jersey number,
     club affiliation, match identifiers);
  2. match video content depicting identified athletes;
  3. derived performance metrics including aggregated and per-rally
     statistics;
  4. authentication and access metadata for authorized Users (account
     identifiers, role designations, session timestamps);
  5. operational telemetry and audit logs.

**Categories of Data Subjects:**

  1. athletes participating in matches recorded through the Software;
  2. coaching staff, analysts, and administrators authorized to operate the
     Software on behalf of the Controller;
  3. third parties incidentally captured in match video to the extent
     specifically authorized by the Controller.

## ANNEX 2 — TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

The Processor shall implement and maintain technical and organizational
measures appropriate to the risk profile of the Personal Data Processed
under this DPA, including without limitation:

  **(a) Encryption in transit.** Personal Data transmitted between the
  Controller's environment, the Processor's infrastructure, and any approved
  Sub-processor shall be encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.

  **(b) Encryption at rest.** Personal Data stored on the Processor's
  infrastructure shall be stored on encrypted media or within encrypted
  database engines.

  **(c) Access control.** Access to Personal Data shall be limited to
  personnel with a legitimate operational need, authenticated through
  individually attributable credentials, and subject to logging.

  **(d) Audit logging.** Administrative access to systems containing
  Personal Data shall be recorded in tamper-resistant audit logs, retained
  for a period sufficient to support security investigations.

  **(e) Vulnerability management.** The Processor shall apply security
  patches to its infrastructure on a schedule appropriate to the criticality
  of the vulnerability and shall conduct periodic vulnerability assessments.

  **(f) Incident response.** The Processor shall maintain a documented
  incident response procedure and shall test the procedure no less than
  annually.

  **(g) Sub-processor due diligence.** Each Sub-processor shall be subject
  to security and privacy due diligence prior to engagement and shall be
  contractually bound to obligations no less protective than those of this
  Annex 2.

  **(h) Backup and disaster recovery.** Personal Data shall be backed up at
  intervals appropriate to the recovery point objective set forth in the
  principal agreement, with encrypted storage and access controls applied to
  the backup repository.

  **(i) Data minimization.** Where the analytical purpose can be served by
  aggregated, pseudonymized, or otherwise minimized data, the Processor
  shall apply such minimization in preference to processing identifiable
  Personal Data.

The Processor reserves the right to modify the foregoing measures provided
that no modification shall reduce the overall level of protection.
